# The live pitch shifter's quirks

The live pitch shifter (`src/deluge/dsp-next/fixed/pitch_shift/`, `src/synth/sources/input/live/`) was rewritten as
dsp-next components bit-exactly: not one output sample changed. What that kept is listed here, each with where it is,
what it does, what it may be, and how to settle it. The code names each quirk at its line (`quirk Qn`).

Settling one is a behaviour change of its own: an A/B render with the quirk fixed, a spectrum or ear check, a decision,
and a re-baseline of the goldens it moves, with that evidence. The goldens that play the shifter are
`tests/integration/golden/presets/{,stereo/}input_pitchshift*`; the workloads and records under `tests/dsp/` pin it at
the component.

| # | Where | What it does | Suspicion | How to settle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | `LivePitchShifter`'s constructor | A new shifter's newer head starts `DIRECT` at ring position 0, not at "now". The buffer is shared and may have run for a while, so until the first hop (32, 2048 or 256 samples) the head plays whatever the ring holds at 0. | Likely bug: a click or stale audio when a pitched note starts on a running input. | Fixed, accepted by ear (2026-11-05): the first block places the head at its own first frame. Only a shifter made on a running input changes: the chord golden `input_pitchshift_chord` (a third of full scale differs over 40 ms at a later note's start) and the two shared-input workloads. |
| Q2 | `HopScheduler::playtime` | Divides the raw distance by `phase` and then by `phase − 1`. The head closes on "now" at `phase − 1` raw samples per output sample, so the playtime is the distance over `phase − 1`; dividing by `phase` as well makes it short by a factor of the rate. | Not a plain bug: a margin. Dividing once lets hops run longer, and the fading older head then passes now far more often (at +7, 48–73 times per workload against 1–2), reading input 8192 frames (186 ms) old: see Q14. | Kept. Fix Q14 first (a crossfade that cannot outlast the older head); only then can the playtime be exact. |
| Q3 | `SlopeFollower`, `percussiveness()` | The previous slope updates every frame, but percussiveness is sampled every 128 frames, so it measures a one-frame change of a heavily low-passed slope rather than the change over the 128 frames. | Intent unclear. | Measure percussiveness on signal 2 both ways; compare which tracks the bursts' onsets. |
| Q4 | `WaveformAligner`, searching back (`reach`) | When less than one ring has been written (`// Is this right?`), the boundary is computed from ring positions that may already have wrapped. | Suspect, early in a stream only. | A spec on a ring under 8192 frames: does the search ever read before the stream's start? |
| Q5 | `HopPlanner::kMinHop` | The minimum hop is 100 samples ("must be 100, not 200 … maybe not anymore?"). | Tuning folklore. | A/B at +24, where it matters. |
| Q6 | `PercussiveSearch` | The best average is compared as a float division. An integer cross-multiplication would decide the same way and keep the path fixed-point. | Accident. | Prove equivalence over the search's range; if it holds, change it with no output change. |
| Q7 | `PlayHead::step`, repitching | A jump of more than 16 taps re-reads only the last 16, with a clamp the comment calls unnecessary. | Performance folklore; the result is right. | Bench with and without the clamp. |
| Q8 | `PitchShiftTuning` | Beyond ±2 octaves the coarse tables clamp, so every larger shift uses the ±2-octave tuning. | Probably intended. | Listen at ±3 octaves. |
| Q9 | `PlayHead::begin_hop` | The newer head's percussiveness entry is set to `kNoPercussivenessEntry` (`0xFFFFFFFF`), so the next cut check always reads afresh. | Intended. | Named; nothing to settle. |
| Q10 | `WaveformAligner` | The early direction flip (when the first step is worse) is tried only when searching forward, before any full direction has been searched. Pitching down starts searching backward and never flips early. | Probably intended. | Spec it; ask if the asymmetry was meant. |
| Q11 | sample reader, interpolation width grows (`Interpolator::widen`) | The resize copies slot i to slot i + offset in rising i (offset = half the growth, count = the old width), so when the offset is under the old width it reads slots it has just written: growing from 8 to 16 taps (offset 4, count 8), slots 4–11 become copies of slots 0–3 repeated instead of the window moving. From 2 to 16 (offset 7, count 2) the ranges do not overlap. | Likely bug, rare: only when the width changes mid-note between two sizes that overlap. | A spec of an 8→16 widen against a moving copy; check which widths the app can actually change between (interpolation_buffer_size gives only 2 and 16, so the overlapping case may be unreachable). |
| Q12 | `PitchShiftTuning`'s coarse tables | At the clamp's top (+24 semitones and above) the coarse position is exactly 2^27, which indexes the last knot and reads the one after it with zero weight: one entry past each 5-entry legacy table. Harmless in value; undefined behaviour in the legacy arrays. | Bug (out-of-bounds read), no audible effect. | Fixed bit-exactly by a guard knot repeating the last (PitchShiftTuning::Tables); nothing to settle. |
| Q13 | scheduler (`HopScheduler::next`) | Once a block has made its first hop, its later segments skip both the pitch-up shortening (a hop the newer head cannot last) and the percussive cut: the old loop's `just_did_hop` was set at the first hop and never cleared within the block. | Measured: harmless either way. Clearing it per segment reaches only shifts where a block holds several hops (+24): the +24 golden changes in 494 samples, all below 16-bit resolution, and heads pass now slightly more often (11 against 9 at +24). | Kept. |
| Q14 | `HopScheduler` with `HopPlanner` | A head can pass now and read the ring 8192 frames back: the fading older head pitching up (at +7 once or twice per workload, up to 62 frames; at +24 up to 4083 frames), and pitching down the newer head at the stream's first hop (up to 26 frames ahead). The crossfade's budget is the next crossfade length, which the hop's tuning changes after it was used to place the hop, and the hurry is computed per segment. | Bug: a burst of stale audio, rare but large at +24. | Make the crossfade end before the older head's run-out by construction (cap the older head's segment at its playtime), place the pitched-down first hop behind now; count overruns with a head-past-now check in hop_scheduler_spec. |
