# The live pitch shifter's quirks

The live pitch shifter (`src/deluge/dsp-next/fixed/pitch_shift/`, `src/synth/sources/input/live/`) was rewritten as
dsp-next components bit-exactly: not one output sample changed. What that kept is listed here, each with where it is,
what it does, what it may be, and how to settle it. The code names each quirk at its line (`quirk Qn`).

Settling one is a behaviour change of its own: an A/B render with the quirk fixed, a spectrum or ear check, a decision,
and a re-baseline of the goldens it moves, with that evidence. The goldens that play the shifter are
`tests/integration/golden/presets/{,stereo/}input_pitchshift*`; the workloads and records under `tests/dsp/` pin it at
the component.

| # | Where | What it does | Suspicion | How to settle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | `LivePitchShifter`'s constructor | A new shifter's newer head starts `DIRECT` at ring position 0, not at "now". The buffer is shared and may have run for a while, so until the first hop (32, 2048 or 256 samples) the head plays whatever the ring holds at 0. | Likely bug: a click or stale audio when a pitched note starts on a running input. | Fixed, accepted by ear (2026-11-05): the first block places the head at its own first frame. Only a shifter made on a running input changes: the chord golden `input_pitchshift_chord` (a third of full scale differs over 40 ms at a later note's start) and the two shared-input workloads. |
| Q2 | `HopScheduler::playtime` | The playtime divided the head's distance by the shift and again by its excess over 1, a margin under a model of input arriving each sample. | A margin, not the model: input arrives a block at a time. Exact under the old model, heads ran out; under the block model (with Q14's reach rule) no head passes now, hops run 20–70% longer pitching up, and crossfades cut off with no reach left fall from 36 to 12 across the workloads. | Fixed, accepted by ear (2026-11-05): the playtime counts the rest of this block at the head's reach, then a block of input per block, to the longest hop. |
| Q3 | `SlopeFollower`, `percussiveness()` | The previous slope updates every frame, but percussiveness is sampled every 128 frames, so it measures a one-frame change of a heavily low-passed slope rather than the change over the 128 frames. | Measured (2026-11-05): the right measure. On the percussive test signal's known onsets, today's one-frame change separates onset entries with an AUC of 0.928; the change over the 128 frames between entries scores 0.743. | Kept. |
| Q4 | `WaveformAligner`, searching back (`reach`) | When less than one ring has been written (`// Is this right?`), the boundary is computed from ring positions that may already have wrapped. | Measured: real, but only after a restart. A hop near the stream's start reaches before the first frame (the aligner, the percussive search, the old head's averages); a fresh input is silent there, but a restart kept the input before the gap. | Fixed: a restart clears the ring and its percussiveness, so before the start is silence either way (waveform_aligner_spec compares a restarted history over noise with a fresh one). |
| Q5 | `HopPlanner::kMinHop` | The minimum hop is 100 samples ("must be 100, not 200 … maybe not anymore?"). | Measured: binds only at +24, on 7.5% of its hops (at every other shift the planned hops are far longer), and since Q14's reach rule it cannot carry a head past now. | Kept. |
| Q6 | `PercussiveSearch` | The best average is compared as a float division. An integer cross-multiplication would decide the same way and keep the path fixed-point. | Equivalent: two different averages over the search's range (at most 64 entries of 0 to 63) differ far beyond a float's rounding, so the comparisons decide alike. | Fixed: the search cross-multiplies totals and lengths; no output changed. |
| Q7 | `PlayHead::step`, repitching | A jump of more than 16 taps re-reads only the last 16, with a clamp the comment calls unnecessary. | Necessary: a jump past the 16-tap window can only feed 16 taps; the clamp keeps the feed inside the window, which the cursor's skip makes good. | Kept. |
| Q8 | `PitchShiftTuning` | Beyond ±2 octaves the coarse tables clamp, so every larger shift uses the ±2-octave tuning. | Intended: ±3 octaves sound acceptable with the ±2-octave tuning (by ear, 2026-11-05). | Kept. |
| Q9 | `PlayHead::begin_hop` | The newer head's percussiveness entry is set to `kNoPercussivenessEntry` (`0xFFFFFFFF`), so the next cut check always reads afresh. | Intended. | Named; nothing to settle. |
| Q10 | `WaveformAligner` | The early direction flip (when the first step is worse) is tried only when searching forward, before any full direction has been searched. Pitching down starts searching backward and never flips early. | Necessary: pitching down, the new head's window ends at the latest input, so there is nothing ahead to search; searching both ways chose the same offset in all 10,000 probed hops. | Kept. |
| Q11 | sample reader, interpolation width grows (`Interpolator::widen`) | The resize copies slot i to slot i + offset in rising i (offset = half the growth, count = the old width), so when the offset is under the old width it reads slots it has just written: growing from 8 to 16 taps (offset 4, count 8), slots 4–11 become copies of slots 0–3 repeated instead of the window moving. From 2 to 16 (offset 7, count 2) the ranges do not overlap. | Unreachable while the widths are 2 and 16. | Fixed: widening moves the window from its far end; no output changed. |
| Q12 | `PitchShiftTuning`'s coarse tables | At the clamp's top (+24 semitones and above) the coarse position is exactly 2^27, which indexes the last knot and reads the one after it with zero weight: one entry past each 5-entry legacy table. Harmless in value; undefined behaviour in the legacy arrays. | Bug (out-of-bounds read), no audible effect. | Fixed bit-exactly by a guard knot repeating the last (PitchShiftTuning::Tables); nothing to settle. |
| Q13 | scheduler (`HopScheduler::next`) | Once a block has made its first hop, its later segments skip both the pitch-up shortening (a hop the newer head cannot last) and the percussive cut: the old loop's `just_did_hop` was set at the first hop and never cleared within the block. | Measured: harmless either way. Clearing it per segment reaches only shifts where a block holds several hops (+24): the +24 golden changes in 494 samples, all below 16-bit resolution. Since Q14's reach rule the skipped checks cannot let a head read past now. | Kept. |
| Q14 | `HopScheduler::next` / `HopPlanner` | A head could pass now and read the ring 8192 frames back (186 ms): within a block now stands still (the block's input is ingested before it renders), but each head played whole segments and the planner placed heads relative to the block's last frame. The fading older head pitching up (at +24 up to 4083 frames), the newer head at the stream's first hops (up to 26 frames pitching down). | Bug: stale audio, up to 23% of full scale at the stream's first hops in the goldens. | Fixed, accepted by ear (2026-11-05): every segment is capped at each sounding head's reach (its distance to the latest frame over its step, less its phase), the newer head's hop ending there and the older head's crossfade hurried to end there; the planner places a head far enough behind the latest frame for the rest of the block. hop_scheduler_spec checks no head passes now. Hops change by 0 to +8% on most workloads (+33% on down 1 and 5). |
