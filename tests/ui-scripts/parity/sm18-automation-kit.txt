# drum and BACK leaves the menu the drum creator opened (the ShellMenu card has no kit presets); with drum row 0
# selected, CLIP opens the row's automation overview and SelectEnc its sound menu, where SHIFT + the LPF frequency
# shortcut selects the row's param in the view. With AFFECT ENTIRE on the same steps reach the kit's own params. Then
# from the clip view, the row's LPF page opened with AFFECT ENTIRE off, AFFECT ENTIRE turned on and CLIP: the view
