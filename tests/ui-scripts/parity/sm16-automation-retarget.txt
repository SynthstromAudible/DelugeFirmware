# ShellMenu: the automation view retargeted from the menu. From the synth clip, CLIP opens the automation overview and
# SelectEnc the sound menu over it; SELECT into the filters' LPF page selects its focused param in the view, the KIT
# and MIDI column buttons others, BACK to the filters list returns the view to its overview, SHIFT + a shortcut
# selects that param. On its page a main-grid press edits the automation and SHIFT + (0,6) toggles interpolation; the
# gold knobs, a gold push with SHIFT, a Select turn with a pad held, X_ENC with a Horizontal turn, a Horizontal turn
# and a Vertical turn reach the view; BACK out of the menu leaves the view on its param. Every snapshot has a 1 s
# window (blinks). Run on the ShellMenu card.
