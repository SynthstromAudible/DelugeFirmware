This is the Deluge Manual – Amadeus Edition by Synthdawg — "more than just a manual: guidebook,
reference, notebook" — converted from the PDF into searchable, linkable pages. The source is the
second draft of the edition for **community firmware C1.0.0**.

Community firmware has changed and added more since C1.0.0; where a page here disagrees with the
community documentation, the community documentation describes current behaviour. Chapter 15 is
still the OS 4.1 Guidebook's community guide until it is converted from this edition.
