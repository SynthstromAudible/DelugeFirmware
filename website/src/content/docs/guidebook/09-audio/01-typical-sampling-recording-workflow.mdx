Deluge provides a number of audio recording, playback and editing functions. Audio clips can be recorded and aligned to the timing of other functions. Audio clips should not be mistaken for samples. Samples are raw sounds which can be used within kits and synths. Samples can also be created in other systems and imported for use in Deluge. Audio can be recorded in song and arranger views.
