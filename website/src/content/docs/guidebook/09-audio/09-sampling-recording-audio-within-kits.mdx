Kits are a great environment for sampling audio. Samples can be recorded and arranged into a kit. Kits can be combination of samples or synth sounds.

<StepFocus />

### Recording an audio sample in to a new kit

<Steps>

1. Input will be line-IN or mic (external or internal). The mic gain can be changed on the input switch.
2. Press :key[Shift + Kit] from clip view to create a blank a kit clip.
3. The browser will open to select a sample from the SD card or a previous last recent recording may by appear on the grid.
4. Press :key[Record]. Button illuminates flashing red and the display indicates ‘RECORDING’ to indicate that recording has started.
5. Press :key[Record] again to end recording. Recording ends and is normalised.
6. The recording is assigned to a row of pads indicated by a green :key[Mute Pad].
7. Press :key[Audition Pad] to play back the sample.

</Steps>

### Recording an audio sample into an existing kit

<Steps>

1. Input will be line-IN or mic (external or internal). The mic gain can be changed on the input switch.
2. Press :key[Kit] from clip view and selected a kit to record into.
3. Press & hold :key[Audition Pad] of blank or selected row + :key[Record].
4. Recording will start. Button illuminates flashing red and the display indicates ‘RECORDING’ to indicate that recording in progress.
5. Press :key[Record] again to end recording. Recording ends and is normalised.
6. The recording is assigned to a row of pads indicated by a green :key[Mute Pad].
7. Press :key[Audition Pad] to play back the sample.

</Steps>

### Setting sampling input monitoring

<Steps>

1. Press :key[Shift + Settings Pad] to open settings menu.
2. :key[Turn Select] to highlight ‘RECORDING’ - recording option.
3. Press :key[Select].
4. :key[Turn Select] to highlight ‘SAMPLING MONITORING’
5. Press :key[Select] to open monitoring options.
6. :key[Turn Select] to highlight:
   - ON: switches monitoring when recording samples on.
   - OFF: switches monitoring when recording samples off.
   - CONDITIONAL (Default): conditional monitoring. Recording from a microphone (internal or external) AND headphones connected, monitoring will occur. Recording using the LINE IN input, monitoring will always occur.

</Steps>

### Slicing audio across a new kit

<Steps>

1. Press :key[Shift + Kit] from clip view to create a new kit clip.
2. The browser will open to select a sample from the SD card or a previous last recent recording may by appear on the grid.
3. To use a previous recorded samples use :key[Select] to scroll through files and folders. To select another file or folder from within the browser, press :key[BackUndo] to back out of the recording files and navigate others. Selections may default to previously selected folder / file.
4. Press & hold :key[Select] while the desired sample is in view / in-focus to open the sampling context sub menu.
5. :key[Turn Select] to highlight ‘SLICE’
6. Press :key[Select]. Number of slices is indicated at which to divide the sample. 16 is default. :key[Turn Select] to adjust, example, to 8.
7. Press :key[Select] to slice the sample across the elected number of pads.

</Steps>
