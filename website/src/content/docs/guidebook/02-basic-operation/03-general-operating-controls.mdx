More in-depth details of the controls will come later. Some of the basic and fundamental controls are summarised here.

<StepFocus />

### Adjusting the main volume level

<Steps>

1. In any view, turn the (OUTPUT LEVEL) gold coloured rotary control.
2. This control adjusts the main output audio and headphone output levels. Care should be taken when adjusting as there is no display or indicator of the current level.

</Steps>

### Adjusting the tempo

<Steps>

1. In any view, turn the :key[Tempo] black coloured rotary control.
2. Tempo changes in incremental +/- 1 BPM Steps. For larger resolution increments press & :key[Turn Tempo] control. This behaviour can be reversed in the settings under the community features, ‘fine tempo knob’ parameter.
3. The display will indicate the tempo change.

</Steps>

### Playing and stopping a song

<Steps>

1. In any view, press :key[Play] the button illuminates green when playing.
2. To stop the sequencer press :key[Play] while its running. The button will be unlit and the sequencer resets to the beginning when play is resumed.

</Steps>

Note that turning the :key[Select] encoder will scroll to navigate menu options. To jump +/-5 options and speed up scrolling use :key[Shift] + Encoder, i.e. Hold :key[Shift + Turn Select]
