Deluge has a high-pass and low-pass filter. Each is configurable with the mode type and has a morph feature. Filter routing can also configurable.

#### HPF - HIGH-PASS FILTER

Allows higher frequencies to pass through the filter and cuts off lower frequencies.

#### LPF - LOW-PASS FILTER

Allows lower frequencies to pass through the filter and cuts off higher frequencies. Deluge filters are 2-pole and 4-pole and 4-pole analog drive with saturation only on the LPF. Poles refer to how ‘steep’ or aggressive the cutoff curve is. This is measured in dB per octave.

#### CUTOFF & RESONANCE

The two parameters common to both the filters provide control over the cutoff frequency and also the resonance (emphasis at the cutoff point).

</div>

{/* /aside fig-7.7-3 */}

MODE: The LPF has 2 Ladder filters, SVF Notch and SVF Bandpass. The HPF contains a HP Ladder filter, SVF Notch and SVF Bandpass. The State Variable Filters have significantly less distortion than the ladder filters.

MORPH: Adjusts smoothly from a setting of 0 at lowpass through bandpass/ notch to highpass at a maximum of 50. This is inverted in the HPF slot so that at 0 is highpass and at 50 it is lowpassed. Morph adds drive to the LP Ladder filters and FM to the HP Ladder. Morph is modulatable.

FILTER ROUTE: Only accessible via the SOUND menu. Adjusts the filter order from HPF to LPF, LPF to HPF, or Filters operate in Parallel.
