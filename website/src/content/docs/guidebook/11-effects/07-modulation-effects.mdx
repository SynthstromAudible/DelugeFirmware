The Modulation Effects - MOD FX, includes 3 types. A chorus, flanger and a phaser. The Mod-FX can also be switched off. Chorus operates similar to a delay which ‘layers’ one or multiple signals with the delay modulated to give a thicker more lush sound. Flangers are similar to chorus with the resonance creating the unique flanging effect. A phaser sits in between the chorus and flanger in sound characteristics where typically a modulated filter delivers its characteristic sound.

#### Mod-FX Parameters

TYPE

Sets the modulation effect type between ‘OFF’, ‘FLANGER’, ‘CHORUS’ or ‘PHASER’. This setting will also designate which of the other parameters are accessible.

RATE

Rate of modulation: 0-50.

FEEDBACK

Flanger and phaser only. Amount of output fed back to the input: 0-50

DEPTH

Chorus and phaser only. Amount of pitch modulation.

OFFSET

Chorus only. Time offset between signal and delay.

### Setting mod-FX

<Steps>

1. :key[Clip] view, ensure the clip is in-focus, example: a kit.
2. Press :key[Shift] + MOD-FX pad, Mod-FX shortcuts are as below
   - :key[Type Pad]
   - :key[Rate Pad]
   - :key[Offset Pad] chorus only
   - :key[Feedback Pad] - flanger & phaser only
   - :key[Depth Pad] - chorus & phaser only
3. For the selected parameter, :key[Turn Select] to adjust the value.

</Steps>

or

1. Ensure focus is clip view for a synth or kit.
2. Access the effects within the menu’s. Press :key[Select].
3. Navigate in the menu to the FX section and press :key[Select].
4. Navigate in the menu to the ‘MOD-FX’ section and press :key[Select].
5. Select either ‘TYPE’, ‘RATE’, ‘DEPTH’, ‘OFFSET’, 'FEEDBACK'. Press :key[Select] for the parameter to select.
6. Adjust the value of the selected parameter by turning :key[Select]

### Setting a kit mod-FX with affect parameters

<Steps>

1. Press :key[Kit] to select. Button lit orange.
2. Press :key[AffectEntire]. Button lit orange.
3. Press :key[ModRateDepth] affect button. Button illuminates orange.
4. Press :key[Upper Gold] to toggle between the chorus, flanger or phaser.
5. Press :key[Lower Gold] to toggle parameters for the lower control between;
   - DEPTH and OFFSET for chorus.
   - FEEDBACK and DEPTH for phaser.
   - FEEDBACK only for flanger.
6. :key[Turn Upper Gold] to adjust the rate. Will adjust LFO 1 without ‘Affect Entire’ selected.
7. :key[Turn Lower Gold] to adjust the selected parameter. Will adjust vibrato without ‘Affect Entire’ selected.

</Steps>

### Setting a song mod-FX with affect parameters

<Steps>

1. Select :key[Song] view. Button lit blue.
2. Press :key[AffectEntire]. Button lit orange.
3. Press :key[ModRateDepth] button. Button illuminates orange.
4. Press :key[Upper Gold] to toggle between the chorus, flanger or phaser.
5. Press :key[Lower Gold] to toggle parameters for the lower control between;
   - DEPTH and OFFSET for chorus.
   - FEEDBACK and DEPTH for phaser.
   - FEEDBACK only for flanger.
6. :key[Turn Upper Gold] to adjust the rate. Will adjust LFO 1 without ‘Affect Entire’ selected.
7. :key[Turn Lower Gold] to adjust the selected parameter. Will adjust vibrato without ‘Affect Entire’ selected.

</Steps>
