### Changing a system parameter setting

<Steps>

1. Press :key[Shift + Select] to access the settings configuration menu.
2. :key[Turn Select] and scroll to the desired parameter. Press :key[Select]
3. :key[Turn Select] when the parameter is in-focus to change its value or setting.
4. Press :key[BackUndo] to back up out of the menu at any time.

</Steps>

### Performing a factory reset

<Steps>

1. Ensure Deluge is switched OFF / powered down.
2. Press & hold :key[Select] and switch power ON to Deluge.
3. The display will show ‘FACTORY RESET’ to confirm reset is complete.

</Steps>
