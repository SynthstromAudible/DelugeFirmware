MODE: The LPF has 2 Ladder filters, SVF Notch and SVF Bandpass. The HPF contains a HP Ladder filter, SVF Notch and SVF Bandpass. The State Variable Filters have significantly less distortion than the ladder filters.

MORPH: Adjusts smoothly from a setting of 0 at lowpass through bandpass/ notch to highpass at a maximum of 50. This is inverted in the HPF slot so that at 0 is highpass and at 50 it is lowpassed. Morph adds drive to the LP Ladder filters and FM to the HP Ladder. Morph is modulatable.

FILTER ROUTE: Only accessible via the SOUND menu. Adjusts the filter order from HPF to LPF, LPF to HPF, or Filters operate in Parallel.
