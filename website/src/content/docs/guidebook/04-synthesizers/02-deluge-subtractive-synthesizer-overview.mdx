Deluge has a subtractive synthesizer as default, with ring modulation mode option.

#### Deluge Subtractive Synthesis

Deluge uses two oscillators. High-pass and low-pass filters and 2 LFO and envelope options. In addition the Deluge synth can use sample WAV files as the audio frequency source.
