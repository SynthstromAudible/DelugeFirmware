Envelopes are used to shape the sound typically over one cycle of the envelope. ENV1 controls volume amplitude by default. In addition it can be patched to other parameters. ENV2 has freely assignable destinations. The envelopes are traditional ADSR (attack, decay, sustain, release) type.

  decay={14}
  sustain={35}
  release={22}

ENV1 attack 47, decay 14, sustain 35, release 22."

When either of the 2 envelopes modulate a parameter other than volume level, it does so with a 'bipolar' behaviour. This means that when the sustain is set to 25 (default for ENV2), that stage of the envelope will match the current setting of the target parameter without modulation. Sustain settings below 25 will then modulate the parameter lower than its current setting, also determined by mod depth. Bipolar behaviour affects all stages of the envelope but is most important when considering sustain level settings, as the others are time based.

The LFO is a continuous modulation function. Wave options are Sine, Saw, Square or Triangle. LFO1 has an additional SYNC parameter to set a sync time interval or switch off. LFO1 does not retrigger and covers all voices. LFO2 is retriggerable and exists for each voice separately.

## Envelope modes and curve <Badge text="C1.4" variant="tip" />

Each of the four envelopes has a MODE and a CURVE setting, found in the envelope's menu after attack, decay, sustain and release (on OLED, on the second page). The other settings, their gold knobs and the envelope graph are renamed to match the mode.

- **ADSR**: the classic attack, decay, sustain and release envelope. This is the default.
- **AHD**: attack, hold at full level, then decay to zero. It always plays through once and ignores the note being released, which suits drums and plucks. DECAY becomes HOLD and RELEASE becomes DECAY, so the release gold knob still sets how long the sound lasts.
- **AR**: while the note is held, the envelope rises to its LEVEL (in place of sustain) at the attack rate. When the note is released it falls to zero at the release rate. With LEVEL at full it's a simple attack/release envelope. If LEVEL is automated or modulated, the envelope glides to each new value instead of jumping, rising at the attack rate and falling at the release rate.
- **MPE**: like AR, but the envelope also follows an expression input, chosen with FOLLOW: PRESSURE (Z), which is MPE pressure or aftertouch, or MOD WHEEL (Y), which is the mod wheel or MPE Y. With no expression it rests at LEVEL, and with full expression it rises all the way.

CURVE (-50 to 50) shapes the attack, decay and release. At 0 each stage is a straight line. Positive values move fastest at the start of each stage, like an analog envelope, and negative values start slowly and speed up. The default of 32 is the shape Deluge envelopes have always had.

In a kit, hold :key[AffectEntire] while changing MODE, CURVE or FOLLOW to change it for every sound row.
