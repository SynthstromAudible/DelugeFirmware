## Description

Step Record lets you enter notes into a clip one step at a time while the
transport is stopped. Instead of recording at the live playhead position, each
note you play lands at a **step cursor**, and the cursor advances one step when
you release the note - so you can build up a sequence deliberately, note by
note, in the style of a step sequencer (KeyStep Pro / MPC style).

It works from the audition pads, the keyboard view, or a MIDI controller, on
both melodic and kit clips.

### Community Feature Setting

Enable the `Step Record (STEP)` community setting (Community Features menu) to
use the mode. It defaults to **Off**.

## Entering and Exiting

- **Enter**: in clip view, hold :key[Record] and press :key[Keyboard]. The clip
  is armed and the cursor starts at the beginning of the clip.
- **Exit**: press :key[Back]. The cursor position is remembered for the next
  time you enter.

While in the mode, the current step shows as a blinking column on the grid (a
tick square on the keyboard screen), the grid auto-scrolls to keep the cursor
visible, and the **Record** and **Keyboard** button lights blink as a reminder
that the mode is active.

## Playing Notes

A note lands at the current step when you press its pad / key, and the cursor
advances one step when you release it.

- **Chords**: notes held and released together land on the same step and
  advance the cursor once.
- **Kit clips**: drums you hit while holding stack on the current step; empty
  drum slots are ignored (add a drum, then re-enter the mode).
- **Velocity**: the audition pads are not velocity-sensitive (they use the
  instrument's default velocity); the keyboard view and MIDI capture live
  velocity.

## Ties and Rests

- **Tie**: while holding one or more notes, press :key[Record]. The held notes
  extend by one step and the cursor advances - so pressing RECORD again while
  holding the note makes it longer. Works on melodic and kit clips alike: a kit
  pad can be a long-lived atmospheric sample, so a held drum note ties too.
- **Rest**: with no notes held, press :key[Record] to advance the cursor one
  step without placing a note - the same press as a tie, but with nothing to
  tie.

## Wrap

When the cursor reaches the end of the clip it wraps back to the start. Placing
a note on a step that already has a note does nothing - the existing note is
kept and the cursor still advances on release.
